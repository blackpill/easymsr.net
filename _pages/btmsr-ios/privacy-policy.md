        <section>
            <h2 class="text-2xl font-bold mb-3">Overview</h2>
            <p>BTMSR is an iOS app for connecting to compatible magnetic stripe readers over Bluetooth and managing card data on your device. This policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is handled.</p>
        </section>
        <section>
            <h2 class="text-2xl font-bold mb-3">Information the app accesses</h2>
            <p>BTMSR uses Bluetooth only to discover and communicate with a compatible reader when you choose to scan or connect. The app may access files that you explicitly select for reading or writing card records.</p>
        </section>
        <section>
            <h2 class="text-2xl font-bold mb-3">How your data is handled</h2>
            <p>Card data and exported record files are processed locally on your device. BTMSR does not sell personal information, use card data for advertising, or transmit card data to our servers.</p>
        </section>
        <section>
            <h2 class="text-2xl font-bold mb-3">Purchases</h2>
            <p>In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. BTMSR does not receive or store your payment card details.</p>
        </section>
        <section>
            <h2 class="text-2xl font-bold mb-3">Contact</h2>
            <p>If you have questions about this policy, contact <a class="underline" href="mailto:btmsr-ios@easymsr.net">btmsr-ios@easymsr.net</a>.</p>
        </section>
