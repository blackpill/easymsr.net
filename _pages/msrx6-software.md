## Bluetooth Setup

1. Turn on the MSRX6/MSRX6BT device.
2. Enable Bluetooth on your phone or tablet.
3. Open EasyMSR and allow Bluetooth access when requested.
4. Select the compatible reader shown inside the app.
5. Test the connection before starting a read or write operation.

Pair the reader from inside EasyMSR when possible. Pairing only through the operating system settings may not establish the application connection.

## MSRX6 Connection Troubleshooting

### The device is not listed

Confirm that the reader is powered on, nearby and not connected to another phone. Restart Bluetooth and reopen EasyMSR.

### Android cannot connect

Check that EasyMSR has the Bluetooth permissions required by your Android version.

### I have an MSR605X USB reader

Use the separate [MSR605X Android USB software page](/msr605x-software/). The Bluetooth and USB products use different connection methods.
